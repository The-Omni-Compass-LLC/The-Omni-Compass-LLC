Seeds 1 to 8, 1.0 day each where a day is run; the standing engine omni-v15, the candidate omni-v16. Evidence class S, our own models: a reading here says whether a change shows promise before a real run, never what a benchmark found. Each candidate reading is paired by seed with the standing engine's (the mean change, its 95% interval over the seeds, the word by the gauge's aim: tools/readings.py).

The candidate is omni-v15 with every clock of the Kubernetes governor counted in seconds and its full decision every 15 s by default (docs/OMNI_V16.md): the brain's minute (omnicompass/adapter.py and its C++ twin), the compass's damping and smoothing, the edge's window and rates (omnicompass/edge.py), the additions past the scheduling floor, a command's minute to land, the host's busy share over a minute (omni_controller/capacity.py), the heat law's minute and one batch job a minute (omni_controller/muscles.py), the coast per raise, the conveyance every 15 s whichever check falls due, the loop that keeps its schedule, and the parser's default periods, the full decision every 15 s and the fast checks every 5 s (omni_controller/controller.py; the founder's word of 22 October, "15 sec all over heart beat"). At a decision every 60 s it reads identical to omni-v15 on every model, every gauge and every seed (the model cluster's audits: 36,922 records over the eight seeds, 4,578 on seed 1, none different). At 15 s, where omni-v15's rules counted in decisions ran four times as fast, every gauge reads STEADY against omni-v15 and the machines parked over the eight days fall from 4,212 to 1,782 (omni-v15 at 60 s: 1,464). As shipped (cluster_shipped: each checkout started without a period, as an install file starts it, omni-v16 at its 15 s against omni-v15 at its 60 s) every gauge reads STEADY. The models that set their own periods (the convey models, the card models, the verdict) read identical. No LOSS against omni-v15. The columns against native read as they read for omni-v15 at the same period: at 15 s the pods waiting for a place (+121.6%) and the 95th percentile (+0.36%) read LOSS against native, as omni-v15's do at 15 s (+111.7%, +0.33%), and at 60 s the same rows read STEADY only by the seeds' spread (+209.8%, +0.63%); with the blink model's alert minutes (+2.35% at 60 s, LOSS in every gate since omni-v13) they are the engine's rows, on the next version's list (docs/CHANGE_CONTROL.md), beside the pace study's (results/model_gate/V16_PACE.md).
