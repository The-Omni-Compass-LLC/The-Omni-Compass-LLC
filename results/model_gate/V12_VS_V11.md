Seeds 1 to 8, 1.0 day each where a day is run; the standing engine omni-v11 (main), the candidate v12-candidate (two fixes). Evidence class S, our own models: a reading here says whether a change shows promise before a real run, never what a benchmark found. Each candidate reading is paired by seed with the standing engine's (the mean change, its 95% interval over the seeds, the word by the gauge's aim: tools/readings.py).

The candidate is omni-v11 with two fixes: a raise the cluster's admission refuses is held until the pod or the raise changes or a wait passes (omni_controller/muscles.py), and the swarm's proximity reflex acts at once with a reach that grows with the square of the speed (tools/run_swarm.py, read on its own simulator in V12_SWARM_REFLEX.md). Against omni-v11 it reads identical on every gauge of every model but one: on the convey model whose every raise is refused, 49.75 commands a day against 1,985.5 (-97.5%, GAIN). The candidate as first pushed also carried the verdict's upper end (a step kept only when its whole 95% interval is inside the allowance); on the cluster models it is shown below each table: no give-back trial could start (about 80 requests a decision, about 4,000 needed a phase), no step was allowed, and the governor read identical to native, omni-v11's machine-hour and energy cuts gone. That rule was taken out of the candidate and kept on the branch v12-verdict-study. The standing engine's own readings against native in these models (the pending pods at 15 s, the blink model's alert minutes, the convey model's start wait) are omni-v11's, unchanged by the candidate, and recorded as in V11_VS_V10.md.
