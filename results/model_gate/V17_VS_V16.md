Seeds 1 to 8, 1.0 day each where a day is run; the standing engine omni-v16, the candidate omni-v17. Evidence class S, our own models: a reading here says whether a change shows promise before a real run, never what a benchmark found. Each candidate reading is paired by seed with the standing engine's (the mean change, its 95% interval over the seeds, the word by the gauge's aim: tools/readings.py).

The candidate is omni-v16 with two changes, both in omni_controller/controller.py (docs/OMNI_V17.md): the parser's default decision period moved from 15 to 60 s (the founder's order of 22 October, afternoon: the heartbeat a dial, the main engine at 60 s), and the cruise wait never fewer than two readings, a fault the dial's ladder found at 90 and 120 s (results/model_gate/V17_LADDER.md); at every period the gate's models run, 60 s and 15 s, the cruise count is what it was, so the fix reads nothing here, and the candidate side, run again on the fixed code, reads the first run's numbers exactly. Every model that sets its own period reads identical to omni-v16 on every gauge and every seed: the model cluster at 60 s and at 15 s, the blink model, the four convey models, the two card models and the verdict. As shipped (cluster_shipped: each checkout started without a period, as an install file starts it, omni-v17 at its 60 s against omni-v16 at its 15 s) every gauge reads STEADY: the machine-hours down 2.30%, the energy per core-hour up 0.49%, the pods waiting for a place up 39.8%, the 95th percentile up 0.27%, the alert minutes up 0.93%, the power cap's moves down 36.0%; over the eight days 1,464 machines parked against 1,782 and 136 give-back trials abandoned against 225. No LOSS against omni-v16. The columns against native read as each period reads them, the same for both engines: at 60 s the pods waiting for a place (up 209.8%) and the 95th percentile (up 0.63%) read STEADY only by the seeds' spread, where at 15 s they read LOSS (up 121.6%, up 0.36%); the blink model's alert minutes up 2.35% (LOSS in every gate since omni-v13); the convey models' pod start wait up 8.99% and its 95th percentile up 10.0% (LOSS in every gate since omni-v8's, the busier host starting pods while it serves 64% more requests); the batch model's requests served down 2.11%, alert minutes up 9.82% and job turnaround up 0.05% (since omni-v13's); the card models' 95th percentile up 2.38% and, near the voltage floor, up 5.78% (since omni-v15's, where they were added; the 99th percentile STEADY, the card's energy down 4.7% and down 1.3%). They are the engine's rows, on the next version's list (docs/CHANGE_CONTROL.md), beside the ladder's (results/model_gate/V17_LADDER.md).
