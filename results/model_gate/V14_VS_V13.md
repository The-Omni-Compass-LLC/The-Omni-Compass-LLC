Seeds 1 to 8, 1.0 day each where a day is run; the standing engine omni-v13, the candidate omni-v14. Evidence class S, our own models: a reading here says whether a change shows promise before a real run, never what a benchmark found. Each candidate reading is paired by seed with the standing engine's (the mean change, its 95% interval over the seeds, the word by the gauge's aim: tools/readings.py).

The candidate is omni-v13 with two changes in the Kubernetes governor (omni_controller/controller.py, omni_controller/muscles.py): `--native` leaves any named muscle where native runs it, never written, an unknown name refused at the start; and the operator's own commands run without a shell unless they hold a pipe, a redirect or a variable (docs/OMNI_V14.md). With no muscle named and no operator's command in any model, both should read identical here, and they do. Since this gate the batch model lists a batch pod as Pending until its container runs (3 s, the rig's own pod start), as a cluster lists it, for both engines. A third change, to the conveyance's reading on a shared host, was read on this same model against omni-v13 and refused (the web's 95th percentile 6.4% higher, LOSS); it is not in the candidate and is kept on the branch v14-convey-study (docs/OMNI_V14.md, section 3).
