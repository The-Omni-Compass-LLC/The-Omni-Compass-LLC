Seeds 1 to 8, 1.0 day each where a day is run; the standing engine omni-v14, the candidate omni-v15. Evidence class S, our own models: a reading here says whether a change shows promise before a real run, never what a benchmark found. Each candidate reading is paired by seed with the standing engine's (the mean change, its 95% interval over the seeds, the word by the gauge's aim: tools/readings.py).

The candidate is omni-v14 with the brain's request for the power cap trimming only while the response line has been clean for the governor's own clear time (omnicompass/adapter.py, its C++ twin; the card governor hands the brain its clear flag), and every wire that holds a governor's rules inside the fingerprint (docs/OMNI_V15.md). Every service gauge of every model reads identical between the two engines, the card models identical (the card governor applied the same gate outside the brain before), and the verdict identical; the request's own moves, reversals and travel, read on the cluster models since this gate, are STEADY (+3.7% at 60 s, −1.4% at 15 s; the model cluster's breaches are rare and short, where the long run's, the fault's regime, clustered: on its own 350 decisions the gate leaves 35 moves of 126, tests/test_cap_band.py). New with this gate: the request's wear on the cluster models, and two card models, the GPU governor of each checkout on the modelled card of tools/gpu_physics_sim.py (the card whose voltage falls with its clock, gamma 3, and the card near its voltage floor, gamma 1.5), native the card's own firmware at its start limit. A dwell with a glide, the first form of the change, was read on this same model against omni-v14 and refused: seven small moves for one on the model cluster and 25 limit writes for 16 on the model card at the same travel; it is not in the candidate.
