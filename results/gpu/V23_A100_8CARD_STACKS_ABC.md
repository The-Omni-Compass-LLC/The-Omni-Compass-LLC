Each run, on an eight-card A100 machine after its pooled card benchmark (docs/GPU_PREREGISTRATION.md, amendment 10), put six organisms each on its own card (the spine was not among them) at 1, 10 and 100 copies until its rented hours' budget of 10.5 hours ended, one paired repetition at each size it reached: the repetitions each run finished are counted beside each row. A size is judged by the one key where every run finished two or more repetitions of it; with one repetition a run it is shown, not judged. The card governor's writes, from each Omni-Compass arm's own audit: A 0 in 15 arms, B 0 in 12 arms, C 0 in 12 arms; it wrote nothing on the card, so on the card the two arms were one system and a change read there is the order of the arms and the card's own drift, not the engine (the stacks' modelled muscles were governed in every arm).
