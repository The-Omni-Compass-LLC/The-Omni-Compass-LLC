The founder's order of 2 November 2026: a referee audit, a consensus and structural audit of the pagination, the tone, the
literature and the documentation, an editorial audit of every structure; nothing outside its cell, standard faces and sizes,
every manual, page and the live engine in line with how Omni-Compass is gauged, what it is and what it does. This page is that
audit's record: what was read, how, what was found, what was fixed in the same push, and what stands open with its reason.

## What was read

The front page, the disclosures, the three manuals (the Operator's, the Owner's and the Live Benchmark Engine Manual, as text
and as built books), the live engine page and its two copies (the word-for-word appendix and the one-file site copy), the
industry landscape page, the budget read by the equation (`docs/WORK_PER_BILL.md`), the work test by test
(`results/WORK_TODAY.md`), the website's remove list (`docs/WEBSITE_CLAIMS.md`), the pilot protocol and kit, `llms.txt`
and the four AI instruction files.

The same day's layout pass, on main before this push (commit f1a88d7ae1), measured the three manuals' pagination and every PDF
page's text against its margins and type sizes, and the live page in a browser at 390 and 1400 px; what it fixed (drawings shrunk
below their 9 pt type, a heading alone at a page's foot, a figure three pages from its text, the live page's time column and grid
label spilling their cells, three heading levels skipped) is in that commit and is not repeated here. Where both passes changed
the same lines, the glove-box card, this push keeps the founder's words whole and changes only the table's shape.

## How it was read

- **Every number against its record.** Each headline figure a page prints was read against the record that writes it: the Omni
  index (`results/OMNI_INDEX.json`), the three arms (`results/orchestrator/V21_THREE_ARMS_ABC.json`), the whole system
  (`results/WHOLE_SYSTEM.json`, regrouped by `docs/engine/export_whole.py`), the two bills (`results/TWO_BILLS.json`) and every
  three-run table that reads the alert minutes.
- **Every page against the seat.** Each statement of where Omni-Compass sits and what it touches was read against the places'
  own words (`tools/places.py`: one mechanism, in one place, the software end, felt along the whole path) and the data-path
  declaration (`DISCLOSURES.md`, section 10).
- **Every figure an outside text carried.** Each figure an outside AI attributed to Omni-Compass (a 2.9% "stack tax", 13.1% on
  bare metal, 5.3% through the cloud, $16 million saved, 112 and 113 units) was searched for on every page; none is carried.
- **The books as printed.** The page fill of every page of the three built books, the figures' overflow record (every word in
  its cell, `docs/book/schematics.py`), the faces embedded in each PDF (`pdffonts`), the formulas in Unicode
  (`tools/unicode_math.py`), the navigation and the stale-engine words (`tools/layout_check.py`), every link and anchor of every
  page (`tools/site_audit.py`), the notice on every file (`tools/legal.py`), the live page's engine against the Python and the
  C++ (`docs/engine/check_live_engine.py`).
