## The look

Base: Photoreal live-action cinema, shot on 35mm film with fine grain, natural motivated light, shallow depth of field, restrained color grade, steady composed camera.

Light, by name:

- `night_storm`: night in a tropical storm: deep blue-black, sodium-amber streetlight, rain streaks catching the light.
- `bedroom_night`: 3 a.m. darkness lit only by cool blue phone and laptop glow, a slow ceiling-fan shadow.
- `day_storm`: overcast pre-storm daylight, flat grey-green, wind moving everything.
- `office_storm`: pewter storm light through glass walls, cool and flat, warm interior practical lamps.
- `ed_dusk`: cyan-white hospital fluorescent light, dusk blue outside the ambulance bay.
- `data_hall`: cool blue-white LED light, black racks, thousands of tiny green and blue indicator lights.
- `generator`: after the power cut: dim amber emergency light mixed with blue monitor glow.
- `ct_room`: cool clinical light, the white ring of the scanner, monitor glow.
- `dawn`: pink-gold dawn after a storm, wet surfaces reflecting, clean air.
- `car_dawn`: pink-gold dawn through a windshield, laptop glow on a tired face.
- `morning_garage`: soft warm morning light through an open garage door, dust in the air.

Signs: status lights are shapes: a green circle means working, a yellow diamond means heading for its line, a red octagon means over its line.
