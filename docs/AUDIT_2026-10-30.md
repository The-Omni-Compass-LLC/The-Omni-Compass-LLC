What it says, plainly. At the factory dial the card benefit is zero: native already runs the card at its power and clock
top, and Omni-Compass's max cannot push past a muscle's limit. The tune-down buys about three-quarters of a percent more
work for each joule, with the same requests served at the same speed. Its energy is down 0.74%, a cut, GAIN. Under the
declared tune rule (`docs/THE_TUNE.md`), a tune that reads no LOSS against native may become the card's factory dial, but
only as the next version and only through the model gate. That is the founder's call, told, not taken. The cap's LOSS rows
come from run A alone, where none of them is clear of the noise. They stay on the record and on the next version's list.

One scoring fault was found and fixed here. The card tool (`tools/gpu_reps.py`) did not list "the share of samples held at
the power limit". It fell through to "work", so a card pinned at its limit less often, serving the same requests, read
LOSS. By the one key of 17 October it is power held: a fall is a cut, a GAIN. It now reads so, and
`tests/test_audit_2026_10_30.py` holds it. No earlier table moves for it: the omni-v18 card table read that row EVEN.

## 4. The founder's week (37 commits, 28 and 29 October): strengthened, and what it cost

Strengthened:

- **The scheduled health monitor works again.** My `tools/repo.py` of 27 October had broken it: its sparse checkout
  lacked the file. Fixed in `00113d9ce`, with a test.
- **The seven-organism grid's checkout.** On the night of 29 October, 245 of 248 jobs failed (run 40818396777). The
  repair of `874f5b042` brought that down to 1 to 6. This audit's fix takes on what was left.
- **A failed arm keeps its evidence.** `scripts/kind_bench.sh` now marks a failed arm `INVALID`, keeps every
  diagnostic, and hashes them all. That is how this audit found the two faults in section 3 from the archived files
  alone.
- **The CPU power runner keeps its exit status and its logs on an early failure** (`b0ba10ab7`, `c04b6b100`).
- **New checks and disclosures.** A paired comparison of good work against its cost (`c01f49b4a`, with tests). Invalid
  offered-request counts are refused (`995214b6b`). The front page now names the limits of a composite number and which
  inputs are modelled (`a7f3760cd`). Claim boundaries and remediation gates (`85ab96bee`).

Cost:

- **The new evidence tool refused good arms** (section 3), fixed here.
- **Churn.** The EVEN band was narrowed to 0.25% and then restored byte for byte to the 0.5% of the founder's order of
  24 October (`46d02de57` … `11ed94992`). Vendor protocol pages were added and removed the same week (seven commits),
  so they net to nothing. The burst of commits is where most of the 31 red verify runs came from.

Nothing in the week changed the engine: no fingerprinted file moved.
