The room is written as a share of the band: 1.0 means native sits at one end and the whole band lies the other way; 0 means native is at that end. Where every knob of a family is at its limit on native the band is one point and the room is none. The wall's kind (design or physics) is the one statement here that is not a measurement: it says what holds the wall, so the work of `docs/THE_ROOM.md` section 5.3 knows which walls a muscle of our own can move.
