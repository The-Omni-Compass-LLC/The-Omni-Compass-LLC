## Fee terms

**1. Savings participation.** Proposed software licensing fee: **25% of verified, attributable net savings** over a specified contractual measurement period, subject to an agreed eligible-cost definition and native baseline.

```text
S = max(0, eligible_native_cost - eligible_omni_cost - excluded_adjustments)
fee_savings = 0.25 * S
```

This is not a 25% reduction in the buyer's bill and not evidence that a 20% saving has been achieved. No amount is payable on a negative savings figure under this proposed formula absent an explicit minimum or alternative fee in a signed contract.

**2. Hardware embedding.** Proposed additional **5% royalty** when the Omni-Compass technology is embedded in, licensed with or monetized through qualifying hardware. The **royalty denominator remains to be negotiated**: for example, qualifying attributable hardware license revenue, not automatically all gross hardware sales. The same transaction may carry separate savings participation and embedding royalties **if the final agreement unambiguously authorizes both**, avoids unsupported legal entitlement assumptions and defines anti-circumvention and measurement provisions.

```text
fee_hardware = 0.05 * contractually_defined_royalty_base
fee_total = fee_savings + fee_hardware + other_signed_fees
```

**3. Extra useful work in the same time.** This is a separate product-performance benefit and possible value-based licensing dimension, not a measured universal percentage. For the same workload and fixed interval `T`:

```text
extra_goodput_pct = 100 * ((successful_omni_work / T) /
                            (successful_native_work / T) - 1)
```

The model cannot infer this from response time or composite indices alone. A separate fee on extra useful work, if desired, must be negotiated with an explicit attributable dollar valuation and cannot simply be added to percentage savings as though the denominators were interchangeable.

## Illustrative economics, not observed results

With eligible savings `S = $20 million`, fee under the **25% proposed saving share** is `$5 million`. If an independent, contractually eligible hardware royalty base is `$100 million`, the **5% proposed hardware royalty** is `$5 million`; combined illustrative amount `$10 million`, provided both fees are authorized on those bases. No empirical Omni-Compass benchmark supports these invented example savings or sales volumes.

## Website and investor disclosure

The public licensing page may state: “**Proposed commercial structure: 25% of contractually verified cost savings, plus a separately negotiated 5% royalty on qualifying embedded hardware revenue.** Additional useful work in the same time is measured and reported separately.” No representation should assert that the percentages are current executed customer contracts, that the full bill falls by 20%, or that entire hardware revenue is necessarily royalty-bearing.

Any final drafting should undergo counsel review for IP ownership and licensing authority, sales tax, accounting, enforceability, definitions, audit rights, non-duplication language and industry-specific hardware obligations.
