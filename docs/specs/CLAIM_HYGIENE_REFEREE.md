## Preserve
- Reproducible seeded execution, explicit finite-horizon predicates, parity receipts, bounded command authority, causal receipts, restoration, and adverse results.
- Scope boundary: explicit state, bounded actuation, measurable feedback, declared admissibility.
- Piece II-IV interpretations only where they reduce to Piece I without adding physical claims.

## Quarantine from engineering proof
- `math = reality`; simulation is not automatically deployment evidence.
- blanket `20-70% lower than existing methods`, `anything that exists`, `obsolete`, or billion-dollar technical conclusions unless a named matched benchmark supplies the evidence.
- probability assignments for untested physics.
- any implication that a diagram establishes control over healthcare, grids, vehicles, robotics, cloud, or other domains.
- any statement that no external supervisor/fallback is permitted when a deployment safety case requires emergency stop, rollback, or independent shield. Safety mechanisms are not competing optimization authority.

## Mathematical caution
A proposed geometric/gradient-flow representation is an interpretation only unless equivalence to the executable Piece-I vector field is proved component by component. A new second-order or field equation cannot simultaneously be called `no new dynamics` unless a reduction/equivalence proof is supplied. The executable eight-line mechanism remains controlling for runtime claims.
