Every number on this page is read from its record when the page is written (`python3 tools/faq.py`); each answer names the page that holds the whole story. Our own models (class S) are never offered as proof; live software is class L and a physical meter class P.

**What is Omni-Compass, in one sentence?** A supervisory governor: one brain that sits on top of the controllers a stack already runs (the Kubernetes autoscaler, a card's power firmware, a database's pools) and turns their settings toward the founder's aim, more work, faster, on fewer machines, never past any part's own limit; it never replaces them and is never in the data path (`DISCLOSURES.md`). The aim is not yet the result: the record reads the work and the speed, and the whole organism's composition reads the machines more, not fewer, power given (`results/WHOLE_SYSTEM.md`).

**What does it show today, in one number?** The Omni index, every real test weighed together: **GAIN 20.4%** (work, speed and machines; its second reading, work and speed alone, GAIN 94.0%), over 26 real tests on GitHub's own machines, on the tables of omni-v25 (`results/OMNI_INDEX.md`). The engine that stands is omni-v25; every benchmark runs again on it, and the index is written again as its tables land.

**Does it do more work for the bill?** The one equation is G = (1 + ΔW) / (1 + ΔB) − 1, the work for each dollar against native (`docs/WORK_PER_BILL.md`). On the chip (one A10 card on Lambda, omni-v23) its three runs read G up 1.5%, up 0.7%, down 0.7%: the runs disagree. Through the cloud (Azure, omni-v18) the set read the bill but not the work, so G is not read there yet; the sets with the work meter are queued.

**Does it take pages away from the people on call?** Over 41 real Kubernetes tests on omni-v25, GAIN in all three of our runs pooled: the minutes an on-call page would be open down 7.4%, from 492.9 to 456.4 a repetition (fewer minutes, fewer pages); test by test fewer in 15 (to none in 3), the same in 2, more in 24; Omni-Compass wrote its own settings with no person touching either arm. What that is worth is the owner's people share times that fall (`results/OVERSIGHT.md`).

**Has it ever done harm?** Every LOSS row is kept and listed: 520 rows read LOSS across their three runs in the newest tables, 273 of them Omni-Compass on top of its own native, each with its cause where one is written and the version that fixed it where one did (`docs/LOSS_LEDGER.md`). A LOSS at the factory tune is a fault to fix, never a number to accept; the open ones are on the next version's list (`docs/CHANGE_CONTROL.md`).

**What does a person still do?** Hold the kill switch (one hand, instant, security only), set each service's response line and choose the profile. Everything else Omni-Compass does on its own: reads every gauge, writes only through the two-way plug, reads each write back, records its reason, and hands every setting back to native at the reset, after a crash gradually (the lease).

**Where does it run, and where does the benefit live?** At the software end, wired in at any of three places: on site, through a cloud, or at the chip. The benefit is made and measured at the software end; at the chip and in the cloud's bill the record is read for whether it holds (a place holds when its runs read no LOSS) and for what the work per joule or per dollar does (`docs/HOW_TO_READ_THE_RESULTS.md`). GitHub's runners are never called bare metal.

**What is not claimed?** No outside party has reviewed or approved these results; no model result is offered as proof; no figure without a record (a "20% drain", a 2.9% "stack tax", a projected percentage) is written anywhere (`docs/WEBSITE_CLAIMS.md`); turning any knob off the factory tune can change every metric, in any direction.

**How do I see it work?** Open the live engine page (`docs/engine/OMNI_COMPASS_LIVE_ENGINE.html`) and press **Guided tour** on the right edge: a recorded voice walks every section, and every stop can be paused, replayed and read along. To put it on a system, the Operator's Manual starts it in watch mode first: it reads every gauge for days and writes nothing (`docs/OMNI_COMPASS_MANUAL.md`, chapter 6).

**What comes next?** Every benchmark's three runs on the engine that stands, the cloud sets that count the work, Google's and Amazon's sets on the founder's word, and the next version's list (`docs/CHANGE_CONTROL.md`).

Run and scored by The Omni-Compass LLC's own code. No outside party has reviewed or approved these results.
