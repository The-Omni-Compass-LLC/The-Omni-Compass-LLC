The four loops in this folder (`dark-1`, `dark-2`, `blue-1` and `blue-2`, each as `.webm` and `.mp4`, with one still frame of
each as `.jpg`) are the moving data centres behind the live engine page (`docs/engine/OMNI_COMPASS_LIVE_ENGINE.html`). They are decoration: they show no
measurement.

**Source.** [B-roll of Wikimedia Foundation servers at CyrusOne in Carrollton, Texas - photographed in 2015](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:B-roll_of_Wikimedia_Foundation_servers_at_CyrusOne_in_Carrollton,_Texas_-_photographed_in_2015.webm),
by the Wikimedia Foundation, via Wikimedia Commons.

These loops are adaptations of that work and are shared under the same licence. They are not part of Omni-Compass, and The
Omni-Compass LLC's notice does not cover them.

**Changes.** Four stretches trimmed from the 720p derivative, each looped with a short dissolve (its last second or so fades
into its first), scaled to 1280 pixels wide at 24 frames a second, re-encoded without sound (VP9 in WebM, H.264 in MP4), one still
frame taken from each (one second in, JPEG), and shown on the page under a dark tint.

| Loop | From (seconds into the source) | Length | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| dark-1 | 496.0 | 9.0 s | moving down a cold aisle between racks (fisheye) |
| dark-2 | 1212.0 | 10.0 s | a drive array's lights |
| blue-1 | 1135.2 | 7.0 s | the backs of servers, blue network cables and port lights |
| blue-2 | 709.2 | 7.0 s | blue network cabling and switch lights |

**Where each plays.** The page is read as six equal bands from top to bottom, and each band has its loop: dark-1, dark-2,
blue-1, blue-2, dark-2, dark-1. The dark loops sit at both ends of the page and the blue in the middle, so the order reads
the same scrolling down or back up. Each band's still frame shows at once and its loop dissolves in over it as soon as the loop's first frame
arrives; the page picks the format the browser plays, and where a browser plays video only after a tap, the reader's first
tap starts it. The dock's Wallpaper switch turns it off; a reader who asks for reduced motion or to save data sees the
still frames only.
