# The live engine's background video

The five loops in this folder (`dc-1` to `dc-5`, each as `.webm` and `.mp4`) are the moving data centres behind the live
engine page (`docs/engine/OMNI_COMPASS_LIVE_ENGINE.html`). They are decoration: they show no measurement.

**Source.** [B-roll of Wikimedia Foundation servers at CyrusOne in Carrollton, Texas - photographed in 2015](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:B-roll_of_Wikimedia_Foundation_servers_at_CyrusOne_in_Carrollton,_Texas_-_photographed_in_2015.webm),
by the Wikimedia Foundation, via Wikimedia Commons.

These loops are adaptations of that work and are shared under the same licence. They are not part of Omni-Compass, and The
Omni-Compass LLC's notice does not cover them.

**Changes.** Five stretches trimmed from the 720p derivative, each looped with a short dissolve (its last second or so fades
into its first), scaled to 1280 pixels wide at 24 frames a second, re-encoded without sound (VP9 in WebM, H.264 in MP4), and
shown on the page under a dark tint.

| Loop | From (seconds into the source) | Length | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| dc-1 | 496.0 | 9.0 s | moving down a cold aisle between racks (fisheye) |
| dc-2 | 1350.0 | 8.5 s | a data hall from above the racks, cable trays and ceiling lights |
| dc-3 | 1212.0 | 10.0 s | a drive array's lights |
| dc-4 | 1135.2 | 7.0 s | the backs of servers, network cables and port lights |
| dc-5 | 709.2 | 7.0 s | network cabling and switch lights |

The page picks one loop for each part of the page as the reader scrolls, in the format the browser plays. The dock's
Wallpaper switch turns it off; under reduced motion it stands still; a reader who asks to save data gets the drawn aisle
instead.
