The loops in this folder are the moving data centres behind the live engine page (`docs/engine/OMNI_COMPASS_LIVE_ENGINE.html`),
each as `.webm` and `.mp4` with one still frame as `.jpg`. They are decoration: they show no measurement.

**Source.** [B-roll of Wikimedia Foundation servers at CyrusOne in Carrollton, Texas - photographed in 2015](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:B-roll_of_Wikimedia_Foundation_servers_at_CyrusOne_in_Carrollton,_Texas_-_photographed_in_2015.webm),
by the Wikimedia Foundation, via Wikimedia Commons.

**Changes.** Thirteen stretches trimmed from the 720p derivative, each looped with a short dissolve (its last second or so
fades into its first), scaled to 1280 pixels wide at 24 frames a second and re-encoded without sound (VP9 in WebM, H.264 in
MP4); some set side by side as three strips or a grid of six tiles, in a wide and an upright layout; one still frame taken
from each loop (one second in, JPEG); on the page, brightened and shown under a light veil.

Each cut starts at the time given; its loop's first frame is its dissolve length later (1.5 s, or 1.2 s for blue-1 and
blue-2), since the loop's last stretch dissolves into its first. `strips_land` / `strips_port`: faces, blueserv and fronts in three strips, the blue in the middle. `wall_land` / `wall_port`:
six tiles, the blue ones in the middle column (wide) or the middle row (upright).

**Where each plays.** The dock's Scenery button chooses among four sceneries, remembered by the browser: **Aisles** (the
default: aisle1, aisle2, blueaisle, bluetray, aisle2, aisle1 over six equal bands of the page, top to bottom, so the blue
sits in the middle and the order reads the same scrolling down or back up), **Video wall** and **Strips** (one composed
loop for the whole page, wide or upright by the screen's shape) and **Racks** (dark-1, dark-2, blue-1, blue-2, dark-2,
dark-1 over the six bands). Each band's still frame shows at once and its loop dissolves in over it as soon as the loop's
first frame arrives; where a browser plays video only after a tap, the reader's first tap starts it. The dock's Wallpaper
switch turns it off; a reader who asks for reduced motion or to save data sees the still frames only.
