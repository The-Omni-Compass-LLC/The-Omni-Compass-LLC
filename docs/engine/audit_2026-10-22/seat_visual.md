## 6. Method and limits

- **Tools.** `vis_audit.py` (the tour: every section and every registered monitor's panel at 1280 and 390, light and dark, with the canvas text hook, the DOM audit and the pixel check), `probe_dom.py`, `probe2.py`, `probe_signs.py`, `probe_colors.py`, `probe_loss.py`, `probe_extra.py`, `verify_new.py`, `verify2.py`, `canvas_pass.py` (the 08:41 build: every monitor's canvas by its page number, the kill switch, the pairs, the tables); all under `scratchpad/audit/`, their logs beside the shots.
- **The builds.** The page changed on disk at 08:41 UTC on 23 October while this seat was under way. Shots prefixed `d1280_`, `p390_`, `l1280_`, `l390_`, `x1280_`, `x390_`, `probe_`, `crop_` are of the 22:05 build; `n1280_`, `n390_`, `v1280_`, `v390_`, `c1280_`, `c390_` of the 08:41 build. Every major is cited on the 08:41 build; the three minors cited on the 22:05 build only (M5's phone quote board, M15's section 10, and part of M1) concern markup and drawing code the newer build did not change (the `.tbl-wrap`, `pre.math` and `drawQuotes` sources are byte-identical between the two files).
- **Read and not read.** Every screenshot named in a finding was read on screen. Shots taken and not read under the rate limit, so no finding rests on them: `n390_top.png`, `n390_s-read.png`, `n390_tradeQuotes.png`, `c1280_lede.png`, `c390_lede.png`, most `c390_m*` and the `v1280_mon_*` panels of monitors 4 to 7, 11, 16, 22, 24 to 28, 37, 41, 42, 45, 46, 48, 50, 54. The phone grade of part 7 rests on the hook alone. The contents (part v) were not captured: the shot script failed twice on its selector, and the grade there is withheld, not given.
- **The hook's limits.** The canvas text hook measures the device-pixel box of each label from the context's transform. For a turned label (the phase planes' "speed v per decision") it reports a false horizontal overrun, and for a column head drawn with `textBaseline = "top"` at y ≤ 4 px it reports a false vertical one; these were discarded by eye. Its font size is the glyph height after the transform; a label squeezed by `fillWords`'s `scale(sx, 1)` reports as under 11 px though its height is 12 px, which is why M6 is written as a squeeze, not a floor breach.
- **The light scheme.** `color_scheme="light"` was passed to Chromium for two full passes; since the page declares no light theme, those shots are the dark page and were used only for M1.
- **What was not done.** No hover states (the market map's and quote board's hover), no drag of the tensor cube, no click into a muscle cell, no run of the Python's 500 runs from the panel's button; the thumbnails' fidelity on the wall was judged at 1280 only; no printing or PDF export.
