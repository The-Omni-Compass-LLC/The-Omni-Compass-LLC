# Omni-Galaxy Multi-Media Studios Platform (with the KAN-IN™ story builder): source

Built and powered by The Omni-Compass LLC · presents · the Omni-Galaxy Multi-Media Studios Platform and its KAN-IN™ story builder. Website: www.omni-galaxy.com

This branch holds the app's source only. It is kept apart from `main` (the Omni-Compass engine) and is never merged into it.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `canon/canon.html` | The whole app in one file: every screen, engine and module, as published (version 266, build 20261128224343). |
| `modules/` | The newest modules, each also inside `canon.html`: the right-edge rail and panel (`krail.js`), levels (`klevel.js`), speed, click and timer (`kspeed.js`), options, layers and libraries (`kopts.js`), humor (`kfun.js`), the cannon jump generator (`kjump.js`), karaoke live (`kkara.js`), the owner's word (`kowner.js`), the video games (`kg4src.js`, built into `kgames4.js`) and their lobby, controller and controls monitor (`kvg.js`), the mobile hits (`kgames3.js`), the fixes from the whole-app inspection (`kfix191.js`), the studio console, rail and Menu order (`kstudio.js`), Omni-Galaxy Social with profiles (`ksocial.js`) the private on-device library (`klib.js`), the centered landing page (`kcenter.js`), the floating remote control (`kremote.js`), the Easel (`keasel.js`) the AI chat (`kai.js`), Projects with every export format (`kprojects.js`), 🔒 Lock & Shuffle with the loop tools (double the loop 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32 bars, copy and paste a bar; `klock.js`) the knowledge library of music, writing, English, psychology, mathematics and games (`kknow.js`), the logo cannon, which lies flat as the hyphen of KAN-IN, turns up to 45° in two ticks, fires a die and turns back down (`kcanshot.js`) and the levels, which open on Everyday every time (`klevel.js`), the 👤 Profile tab with the birth date that picks the sign and the compass letter and the switch that lets the compass follow the profile or not (`kprofile.js`), the AI's hands: actions the AI can run in the app (`kai.js`), and the console's inputs: drum pads A1–A16 on channels 1–16, every other source wired by drop-down into channels 17–24, MPC-style real-time or latched pad recording, and the click's subdivision (`kio.js`). Every cannon in the opening game and on the landing fires one die as its bullet (`build/dicepatch.py`). Karaoke takes are layers with mute, solo, level, record-over and bounce (`kkara.js`). From version 255: the beat link, which lets the cannon and the games play and record the beat machine on the beat, and the remote's tools on every screen (tempo, volume, upload, save page, library, sounds, language) (`kv255.js`); Cannon Beat, the game that builds the beat (`kcbeat.js`); Explore a religion, the branches, texts, holy days, holy places, practices and sites of the ten most widely known religions (`kreligx.js`); and Learn more links on every topic card (`kweb.js`). From version 256: the floating remote is retired (its transport lives in the pads, keyboard, sequencer and karaoke); the tools sit under 🛠 beside each screen's Undo and Close (`kv255.js`); Alt shortcuts open every screen and tab on any keyboard layout (`kkeys.js`); and LuvMyZodiac is headed by a live zodiac ring of the twelve signs with the Sun, Moon and planets where they are now, with its own full live page (`kzring.js`). From version 257: the app checks itself with 🧑‍⚖️ Referee audit (`kaudit.js`), loads the newest version by itself when one is published (`kautoupd.js`), and runs leaner: the retired remote's repaint loop stands down and the busiest repaint timers run less often, cutting idle work by 60% and start-up time nearly in half. From version 258: 💃 Dance Karaoke, which records a dance to the beat, the DJ deck or an uploaded song and plays it back in sync (`kdance.js`), and 🌐 the Doorway to 57 outside platforms with one search across nine of them (`kdoor.js`). |
| `brand/` | The Omni-Galaxy Multi-Media Studios art: the lightning wordmark cut out for any background (`omni-galaxy-logo-transparent.webp`, used on the landing page, every footer, the Menu, Legal & Safety, Services, Sports, News, the Stock Market and the Knowledge Library), the full banner in its starfield (your page and the social pages), the galaxy wheel with the logo (the agreement card at each visit), the galaxy wheel alone (LuvMyZodiac and the default profile cover) and the tab icon. |
| `build/` | The build: `addmod.py` puts a module into `canon.html` (`addmod2.py` replaces one in place, keeping the load order); `mkall.sh` builds the release into `pub/`. The voice recordings and the VCSL sample library are large and live in the published build, not here. |
